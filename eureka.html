02 — EVENT STRUCTURE & FLOW

THE EUREKA
JOURNEY.

1

Pre-Event Planning

20-member organizing team conducted 5 planning meetings over 2 weeks. Faculty coordination maintained throughout preparation.

2

Marketing & Promotion

Posters, WhatsApp, Instagram/social media, classroom and departmental outreach, and direct student interaction.

3

Registration & Verification

34 teams registered via Google Form. Details verified and participant database maintained. 33 teams participated.

4

Venue & Technical Setup

Auditorium prepared with seating, stage, backdrop, banner, registration desk, projector, screen, microphone, and sound system.

5

Inauguration & Briefing

Welcome address, formal inauguration, judges introduced, competition rules, pitching format, time limits, and judging criteria briefed.

6

Main Competition

33 teams pitched for 5 minutes each, followed by 7-minute Q&A with judges. Single-round direct evaluation format.

7

Judges' Evaluation

All 33 teams evaluated on overall performance, idea quality, communication, and Q&A responses. Direct selection of winners.

8

Results & Prize Distribution

1st, 2nd, and 3rd Place announced by judges, followed by prize distribution ceremony.
