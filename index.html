ENTREPRENEURSHIP.

E-Cell at V.S.B. Engineering College creates a platform for students to explore entrepreneurship, develop innovative ideas, build prototypes, participate in competitions and connect with mentors and industry.

THE JOURNEY

FROM IDEA TO IMPACT.

01

IDEATE

Identify problems and explore opportunities through structured brainstorming and design thinking.

02

VALIDATE

Understand users, markets and feasibility. Test assumptions with real-world feedback and data.

03

BUILD

Create prototypes and MVPs. Transform concepts into tangible products and services.

04

PITCH

Present ideas and receive feedback. Sharpen your storytelling and investor readiness skills.

05

MENTOR

Learn from founders and experts. Gain insights from experienced entrepreneurs and industry leaders.

06

LAUNCH

Take ideas into the real world. Deploy, scale and make a measurable impact in your community.
